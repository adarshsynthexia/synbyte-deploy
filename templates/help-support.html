Help & support

A quick guide to using the SYNBYTE student prototype.

For consumers

1. Sign in as a consumer.

2. Browse the menu, add food from one stall and check out.

3. Complete the clearly labelled test payment. A token is issued by the backend only after simulated success or captured gateway test payment.

4. Track the order and show your token once marked Ready.

Consumer login

For vendors

1. Sign in with a vendor account.

2. Open New Orders and move them through Accepted, Preparing and Ready.

3. Check the customer's token at the pickup counter and complete collection.

Vendor login
This is a local educational prototype. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by LPU or any of the sample food vendors. Live payments and real food availability are not enabled by default.
